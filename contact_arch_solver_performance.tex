\section*{Contact-Arch Solver Performance}

This report summarizes the unified current-code, output-enabled contact-arch
response matrix run on \texttt{rzwhippet53} on 2026-08-19.  The authoritative
full-output matrix is
\path{build-rzwhippet-toss_4_x86_64_ib-llvm@19.1.3-release/paper_contact_arch_response_20260819_124814}.
Commands, logs, response histories, executable and source identity, status,
validation, plots, and selected field renders are archived under
\path{/usr/workspace/tupek2/dev/buckling_solver_paper/results/contact_arch/solver_response_20260819_124814}.

\section*{Configuration}

The three-dimensional model uses the first-order
\path{half_circle_arch_3d.g} mesh.  The arch is Neo-Hookean with density 1,
Young's modulus 10, and Poisson ratio 0.33.  Its supports move inward in the
$x$ direction while remaining fixed in $y$ and $z$.  The calculation advances
through 50 equal increments from time zero to 0.16, giving a final inward
displacement of 0.008 at each support.

Contact is represented by a custom smoothed penalty boundary integral on the
outer arch surface, not by Tribol mortar contact.  A descending upper plane and
a fixed lower plane define the environment.  The penalty parameter is 20, the
regularization length is $10^{-3}$, the initial upper-plane clearance is
$10^{-4}$, and the upper plane travels 0.032 over the reported schedule.

All eight entries use eight MPI ranks, cold starts, CG, nonlinear tolerance
$10^{-11}$, and linear tolerance $10^{-14}$.  The nonlinear iteration cap is
10000.  Hypre AMG uses a 600-iteration CG cap, and Hypre Jacobi uses a
60000-iteration cap.  Both BSR SpMV and assembled BSR are disabled.  Every
entry writes the initial response and field state plus each accepted
increment.  The 1800-second outer limit was selected because an earlier
20-minute diagnostic run stopped both AMG trust-region entries after 42--44
accepted increments.

\section*{Completion and Timing}

Only baseline and safeguarded-subspace trust region with Hypre AMG complete
all 50 increments.  The Jacobi trust-region variants fail late, after 43 and
44 accepted increments.  Newton-CP fails after eight or nine accepted
increments, and Newton-LS fails after four or eleven.  Every stored response
state has a corresponding eight-piece visualization cycle with all required
displacement and reaction fields.

is the sum of the iteration counts reported for each attempted increment,
including the final unsuccessful increment.

\section*{Response Validation}

Validation confirms the prescribed time, support-inset, and upper-plane-travel
schedules to stored precision.  The globally reduced support reaction is
finite and nonnegative in every retained state.  The two completed histories
contain 51 response states and 51 field cycles.  They have identical stored
support reactions and differ by at most $10^{-8}$ in average contact-surface
displacement.

The completed response reaches a peak support reaction of
$2.69340\times10^{-4}$ at support inset 0.0072 and upper-plane travel 0.0288.
The reaction then decreases to $2.40731\times10^{-4}$ at the final inset 0.008
and plane travel 0.032 while the average contact-surface displacement reaches
$-1.28791\times10^{-2}$.  The turnover and accepted-state sequence accompany
the large arch inversion rather than defining a validated contact threshold.

\section*{Solver Diagnostics}

Newton-CP with AMG stops on increment 9 with PETSc reason $-4$, residual
$4.86\times10^{-4}$, and 40 indefinite-CG diagnostics.  With Jacobi it stops
on increment 10 with reason $-2$, residual $2.32\times10^{-5}$, 154
indefinite-CG diagnostics, and one inner-CG cap hit.

The other four failures reach the 10000-iteration nonlinear cap.  Newton-LS
ends with residuals $1.36\times10^{-10}$ for AMG and
$1.15\times10^{-5}$ for Jacobi.  Baseline and subspace trust region with
Jacobi end with residuals $1.23\times10^{-10}$ and
$2.27\times10^{-9}$, respectively.  Neither completed AMG trust-region entry
reports an indefinite-CG diagnostic or an inner-CG cap hit, although their
largest per-increment nonlinear counts are 7779 and 7851.

\section*{Interpretation and Limitations}

This matrix supports a narrow robustness conclusion: under the common solver
contract, the two trust-region configurations with AMG are the only entries
that complete the strongly nonlinear loading path.  Preconditioner choice
changes completion even though all methods follow the same stored branch on
their shared accepted states.  The safeguarded subspace neither reduces work
nor changes completion for AMG, although it advances one step farther with
Jacobi.

The case does not certify stability of the accepted states, validate a contact
formulation, or establish penetration accuracy.  The imposed plane is not
written as field geometry, and the output contains no explicit contact-gap,
penetration, or penalty-traction field.  Contact engagement is therefore
interpreted from the known plane schedule, globally reduced support response,
and deformation sequence rather than independently validated.  The case has
no analytic reference, accepted-state eigenanalysis, load-step study, or
penalty-sensitivity study.  It should appear in the paper only as a robustness
and conditioning example.
