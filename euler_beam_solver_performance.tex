\texttt{rzwhippet7} on 2026-08-13.  The authoritative matrix is
\path{build-rzwhippet-toss_4_x86_64_ib-llvm@19.1.3-release/paper_euler_cylinder_response_20260813_110039}.
The compact commands, logs, response histories, and shared status table are archived

reported lateral displacements $-0.00382636$ at force $0.00274$ and $-1.35237$
at force $0.00275$.

\section*{Configuration}

The matrix uses the standard unrefined 1400-element quadratic beam mesh, four MPI
ranks, 30 coarse load steps followed by five refined steps, and a weak lateral
selector traction of $-10^{-10}$.  The applied compressive resultant reaches
$2.75\times10^{-3}$.  This standard matrix remains distinct from the one-level-refined
11200-element run used for the analytic Euler-load comparison.

\section*{Response Branches}

All eight histories remain nearly coincident through applied force
$2.74\times10^{-3}$, where the average lateral top displacement is approximately
$-3.238\times10^{-3}$.  Newton-LS spends the remainder of its one-hour allocation on
the final increment and does not accept a state at $2.75\times10^{-3}$.  Newton-CP and
both trust-region variants accept that increment and select the same large-deflection
branch: their final average lateral displacements range from $-0.522135$ to
$-0.522400$.  Differences between AMG and Jacobi are negligible for Newton-CP and at
most $2.6\times10^{-4}$ in average lateral displacement for the trust-region runs.
