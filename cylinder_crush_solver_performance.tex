\section*{Configuration}

The matrix uses \path{thin_cylinder_crush.g}, first-order displacement elements,
16 MPI ranks, and 16 prescribed-displacement increments to total top compression
$0.07$.  The nonlinear tolerance is $10^{-11}$ and the linear tolerance is
$10^{-14}$.  Hypre AMG uses a 600-iteration CG cap, and Hypre Jacobi uses a
60000-iteration cap.  The eigenpair matrix used the same discretization, loading,
tolerances, and 16-rank layout and wrote the lowest mode for every final state.

\section*{Reaction Validation}

The reaction helper now maps boundary local vector DOFs to owned local true DOFs,
sums only owned entries, and performs one global reduction.  Every corrected history
contains 17 finite states, starts with zero reaction at the unloaded state, has a
nonconstant reaction column, and ends at average top displacement $-0.07$.  The eight
files are not all identical.  These checks replace the invalid constant
$8.74064\times10^{245}$ histories from the earlier rerun.

\section*{Response Branches}

The eight curves coincide through increment 15.  At average top displacement
$-0.065625$, every run has reaction $-5.87722\times10^{-3}$.  The final increment
separates three response branches:
\begin{itemize}
  \item both Newton methods with both preconditioners end at
        $-6.252508\times10^{-3}$;
  \item baseline trust region with both preconditioners and subspace trust region with
        AMG end at $-2.651752\times10^{-3}$;
  \item subspace trust region with Jacobi ends at
        $-2.224820\times10^{-3}$.
\end{itemize}
The corrected reaction histories therefore agree with the existing deformation
comparisons: the Newton methods remain on the same nearly axisymmetric branch, while
the trust-region methods select buckled final configurations.

\section*{Accepted-State Stability Evidence}

The matched deformation composites show that Newton-LS and Newton-CP converge, but
remain on nearly axisymmetric final states.  The trust-region variants converge to
visibly buckled final states.  The reaction histories split at the final increment in
the same way: both Newton methods end at the higher reaction branch, while the
trust-region methods move to lower-reaction buckled branches.  This is the expected
signature of Newton convergence to an unstable equilibrium branch in this unperturbed
shell problem.

The nonlinear-solve logs provide an additional curvature diagnostic.  The four Newton logs contain CG
messages that the tangent operator is not positive definite.  The most negative
reported $(Ad,d)$ values are $-5.13\times10^{-11}$ and $-3.61\times10^{-8}$ for
Newton-LS with AMG and Jacobi, and $-2.48\times10^{-9}$ and $-1.80\times10^{-8}$ for
Newton-CP with AMG and Jacobi.  No corresponding diagnostic appears in the four
trust-region logs.

The additional matrix directly tests the accepted equilibria.  After the final
increment, the driver rebuilds the tangent in the accepted deformed configuration,
eliminates the 1,536 prescribed true degrees of freedom, and verifies symmetry of the
45,696-degree-of-freedom reduced tangent.  SLEPc then computes its algebraically
smallest eigenpair using Jacobi--Davidson with a matrix-norm-scaled convergence test.
The sign tolerance is $10^{-8}\max(1,\lVert K\rVert_\infty)$, approximately
$1.4\times10^{-8}$ for these states.

All four Newton combinations independently recover the same resolved negative mode,
regardless of Newton variant or preconditioner.  No negative mode is resolved for any
trust-region final state at the stated tolerance.  Their smallest eigenvalues are
numerically zero, as expected from the rotational degeneracy of an unperturbed
cylinder: rotating a buckling pattern changes its phase without changing its energy.
The result should therefore be stated as absence of a resolved negative mode, not as
strict positive definiteness.

Confidence in the branch interpretation was developed in layers.  First, the matched
deformations separated nearly axisymmetric Newton states from buckled trust-region
states.  Second, the corrected reaction histories separated those branches only at the
final increment.  Third, the Newton nonlinear solves repeatedly detected negative
curvature.  Finally, the accepted-state eigenanalysis directly resolves a negative
Newton tangent mode and no negative trust-region mode.  The agreement of these
independent observations supports the unstable-Newton-branch interpretation while
retaining the symmetry and discretization qualifications of the benchmark.
